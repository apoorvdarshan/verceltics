# Verceltics Code of Conduct

## Our community

Verceltics welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Help make
the project a respectful place to ask questions, report problems, share ideas,
and contribute, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or technical experience.

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, project discussions,
the official Verceltics Discord server, and interactions where someone represents
the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and listen to other views.
- Respect people's boundaries, privacy, and decisions about sharing personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant and use the appropriate support or contribution channel.
- Accept corrections, take responsibility for mistakes, and help resolve disagreements calmly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or deliberate personal attacks.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or sexualized content directed at others.
- Publishing private information, messages, credentials, or account details without permission.
- Spam, scams, impersonation, malicious links, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Repeated unwanted contact, evading moderation, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Reporting a concern

Email **[ad13dtu@gmail.com](mailto:ad13dtu@gmail.com)** privately with the subject
“Verceltics conduct report.” Include the relevant context, message or issue links,
and screenshots if available. Do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue or
Discord channel.

Apoorv Darshan, the project maintainer, reviews conduct reports. Reports and
personal information will be handled discreetly and shared only as needed to
understand and address the concern. Retaliation is not permitted.

For software vulnerabilities or exposed credentials, use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For ordinary app bugs or feature requests, see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#reporting-issues-and-requesting-features).

## Moderation

The maintainer may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response should reflect
the severity, context, and any repeated behavior; serious misconduct may warrant
an immediate restriction.

People affected by a moderation decision may request a review by replying to the
maintainer privately with relevant context. These expectations apply to the
maintainer and contributors as well as community members.
