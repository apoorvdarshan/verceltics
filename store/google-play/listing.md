## Full description

```text
Verceltics brings your web stack to your phone. Check projects, zones, sites, search performance and speed audits from one native Android app, without opening five dashboards.

WHAT YOU CAN CONNECT
• Vercel — projects, deployments and Web Analytics
• Cloudflare — accounts, zones, Pages projects and Workers
• Netlify — sites, deploys, domains and build history
• Google Search Console — clicks, impressions, queries, sitemaps and URL inspection
• PageSpeed Insights — Lighthouse mobile and desktop audits plus Chrome UX field data

BUILT FOR QUICK CHECKS
• Search across projects, zones and sites
• Pull to refresh, with saved data shown while you are offline
• Light, dark and system themes
• Explore everything first with sample data, no account needed

VERCELTICS PRO
Browsing your connected accounts is free. Verceltics Pro unlocks project analytics, site and resource details, Search Console reports and the full PageSpeed breakdown. Choose monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled in Google Play.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Tokens and API keys are encrypted on your device with Android Keystore
• Requests go directly from your phone to each provider's API
• No ads, no cross-app tracking, and no Verceltics servers in between

More hosting platforms, registrars and site services are on the way. Verceltics is also available on iPhone and iPad.

Verceltics is open source: github.com/apoorvdarshan/verceltics

Verceltics is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Vercel, Cloudflare, Netlify, Google, or any other supported provider. All provider names and marks belong to their respective owners.
```
