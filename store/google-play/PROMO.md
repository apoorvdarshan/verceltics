# Android beta offer: VERCELTICSBETA

Created in Play Console (Monetize with Play → Promo codes) on October 10, 2026.

## Share with testers

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Offer | 30 days of Verceltics Pro free on the Monthly subscription |
| Promo code | `VERCELTICSBETA` |
| Valid | October 10, 2026, 6:31 PM until December 31, 2026, 12:00 AM (Play Console time) |
| Redemptions | Up to 2,000 (the minimum Google allows for a custom code) |

Redeem in the Google Play Store app: profile → **Payments & subscriptions →
Redeem code**, or open https://play.google.com/redeem?code=VERCELTICSBETA on the
device. Then open Verceltics; if Pro does not appear right away, use
**Restore purchases** in the paywall.

After the 30 free days the subscription **renews at the regular monthly price**
unless the tester cancels it in Google Play before the free period ends. This
differs from the iOS code `VERCELTICSPH`, which does not renew
(see [the iOS offer](../../marketing/product-hunt-2026-10/PROMO.md)).
