Verceltics brings your web stack to your phone. Check projects, zones, sites, search performance and speed audits from one native Android app, without opening five dashboards.

WHAT YOU CAN CONNECT
• Hosting (10) — Vercel, Cloudflare, Netlify, Railway, Render, DigitalOcean, Heroku, Fly.io, Firebase Hosting and AWS Amplify: projects, deployments, services and logs
• Registrars (8) — Name.com, Namecheap, Porkbun, Spaceship, Dynadot, NameSilo, Gandi and GoDaddy: domains, expiry, auto-renew, locks and nameservers
• Site services (9) — Google Search Console, Google Analytics, PageSpeed Insights, Bing Webmaster, Microsoft Clarity, Plausible, Umami, UptimeRobot and Better Stack: search, traffic, speed and uptime

BUILT FOR QUICK CHECKS
• Search across projects, zones and sites
• Pull to refresh, with saved data shown while you are offline
• Light, dark and system themes
• Explore everything first with sample data, no account needed

VERCELTICS PRO
Browsing your connected accounts is free. Verceltics Pro unlocks project analytics, resource and domain details, site service reports, provider dashboards and the full PageSpeed breakdown. Choose monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled in Google Play.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Tokens and API keys are encrypted on your device with Android Keystore
• Requests go directly from your phone to each provider's API
• No ads, no cross-app tracking, and no Verceltics servers in between

Verceltics is also available on iPhone and iPad.
